## 📌 Ready-to-Publish LinkedIn Post Copy

Most AI automation projects stop at a toy prototype: a flashy UI with mock buttons, brittle webhook scripts, or an LLM that invents accounting numbers.

Over the past weeks, I engineered something built for true enterprise resilience:

Proud to announce the **Enterprise AI Automation Suite** — a full-stack, production-grade operations system that decouples four mission-critical business automation workflows into independent, verifiable, and secure engines:

---

### 1️⃣ Multi-Source Lead Routing & Instant AI Qualification
⚡ Ingests leads from web forms, webhooks, and CRM sources.  
⚡ Executes deterministic Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) scoring combined with structured LLM fit analysis.  
⚡ Guaranteed SLA: Benchmarked at **310 ms** end-to-end processing (< 10s SLA target).  
⚡ Dispatches verified updates to **Airtable** and **Slack** with built-in idempotency deduplication.

### 2️⃣ Autonomous Customer Support & Intelligent Email Triage
📧 Classifies incoming customer inquiries across 8 distinct intent categories.  
📧 Grounded RAG retrieval referencing an approved corporate knowledge base — zero fabricated answers.  
🛡️ Active multi-tier prompt injection defense neutralizing adversarial overrides.  
🛑 **Strict Draft-Only Operation**: Sensitive actions (refunds, cancellations, credential changes) strictly route to a human approval queue before sending.

### 3️⃣ AI Vision Invoice Scanner & Accounting Sync
📄 Magic byte binary verification (`%PDF-`) blocking polyglot and disguised executables.  
🔍 Hybrid regex and vision OCR extracting line items, vendors, tax, and totals.  
⚖️ **Deterministic Financial Integrity**: Mathematical invariant verification ensures:
$$\sum \text{Line Items} == \text{Subtotal}$$
$$\text{Subtotal} + \text{Tax} - \text{Discount} == \text{Grand Total}$$
📊 Synchronizes approved ledgers directly to **Google Sheets** with transaction rollback and retry safety.

### 4️⃣ End-to-End Multi-Agent Enterprise Operations
🤖 **9 Specialized Autonomous Agents** (Intake, Lead Intel, Support Triage, Invoice Parsing, Contract Templating, Payment Verification, Slack Collaboration, Reporting, and Workflow Supervisor).  
🔄 Coordinated via an explicit Finite State Machine (FSM) preventing invalid business transitions.  
🔐 **Cryptographic Stripe Webhook Verifier**: Deterministic HMAC-SHA256 signature validation with a 300-second replay attack protection window.  
📈 Weekly executive digest synthesized strictly from database facts without hallucination.

---

### 💡 Core Engineering Principles Behind the Architecture:
- **Zero Hallucinated Financials**: AI assists extraction; deterministic code verifies arithmetic.
- **Human-in-the-Loop Safeguards**: Draft-only default for outgoing emails and unreviewed contracts.
- **Dual-Mode Adapter Architecture**: Seamless toggle between Demo Mode (isolated mock fixtures) and Live Mode (real OpenAI, Stripe, Slack, Airtable, Sheets, Gmail APIs).
- **Dual Automation Blueprints**: Exportable visual scenarios for **Make.com** and self-hosted workflows for **n8n**.
- **100% Test Coverage**: 20 automated unit, integration, and security tests running in 3.15s.
